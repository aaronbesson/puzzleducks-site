Privacy Policy

Puzzle Ducks · Last updated November 14, 2026

Puzzle Ducks ("the app") is made by Aaron Besson ("we", "us"). We built the app to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what that means.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: levels unlocked, stars, coins, booster counts and your sound, music and haptics settings. This data never leaves your device (other than as part of your own device backups) and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (such as the Farm Pass, coins and tool packs). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives a record from Apple confirming what you bought, so it can unlock it on your devices. Coins and tools have no real-world value.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app sends your scores (stars, levels cleared, Daily Puzzle results and streaks) and achievement progress to Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple and other players may see your Game Center nickname and scores. We do not receive or store this information. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app at any time.

TestFlight and the App Store

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this only to fix bugs and improve the game. Apple's own handling of data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
